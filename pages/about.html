About KTU Connect

Your simple hub for KTU notes, PYQs and study resources.

What is KTU Connect?

KTU Connect is a student-focused platform created to make Kerala Technological University study materials easier to find and access.

The platform brings together notes, previous year question papers, syllabus information, semester resources and useful academic materials in one place.

What You Can Find

  • KTU Notes
  • Previous Year Question Papers
  • Semester-wise Resources
  • Syllabus Information
  • KTU Updates
  • Study Materials

Our Goal

Our goal is to help KTU students save time searching for study materials and make academic resources easily accessible.
